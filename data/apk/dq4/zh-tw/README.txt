Put here the 3 APK files of Dragon Quest IV taken from your phone. The game as published also holds its texts in Japanese, Korean and Chinese, but the port does not show them yet: the game runs in English, the port's own texts in this language.
    base.apk, split_config.arm64_v8a.apk, split_UnityDataAssetPack.apk
The language chosen in the Esc menu decides which folder is read; without APK files in its folder, the English ones (en-us) are used, otherwise the French ones (fr-fr).
